## Why this one matters more than its size suggests

L&E sells **carports and concrete** from a Temple address. The comparison premise in
`src/components/sections/WhyTripleJ.tsx` — *"leave you to hire someone else for the concrete"* — is aimed at
national kit dealers, where it holds. Against L&E it does not: they pour slabs themselves.

They are the closest local match to Triple J's turnkey model. They do not *market* it as turnkey, do not
differentiate welded work, and are open seven days — which is the one availability edge they hold over
Triple J's Mon–Sat.

Note that `src/lib/competitors.ts` already lists them as `le-metal` in the local roundup, so this is a
publishable, already-public competitor — not new intel.
