# Competitor: Wolf Steel Buildings

> Configurator teardown, 2026-10-17. Julian saved the page; Claude walked the live configurator on a desktop and a phone, looking only (no quote sent). Written for the `/build` step redesign. Not filled from the full template: identity, reviews and SEO are still open.

## Identity
- **Website:** wolfsteelbuildings.com; configurator at design3d.wolfsteelbuildings.com.
- **Positioning:** "Red Iron" buildings. Their header shows a toll-free office number and a quotes@ email.
- **Configurator software:** IdeaRoom, a white-label 3D configurator sold to steel-building dealers. Expect the same layout on other dealers' sites.

## Pricing
- **Prices shown publicly:** no. The configurator says "Please call for pricing."
- **Online configurator:** yes.

## How the configurator works
- **Sections, not steps.** The right panel stacks open-in-any-order sections: Style, Sides & Ends, Size, Interior, Doors & Windows, Materials, Colors, Details. There's no "Next".
- **Opening a section highlights that part of the building.** Doors & Windows outlines the chosen wall in blue with a "Right Wall" label. Sides & Ends outlines the section in orange. A hint over the 3D view says to click the wall or item to change it, so you can edit by tapping the building.
- **Doors by wall.** Pick a wall (Front, Left, Back, Right), then tap tiles to add: Door, Window, Frameout, Roll-up Door, Vent, Overhead Doors. Then "Arrange items" or "Clear". Items drag along the wall, with a note that the layout may change for construction reasons.
- **The Quote button is always in view:** top of the panel on desktop, a sticky bar on phones, with a heart to save the design.
- **A delivery-location box blocks the page on open** until you pick a state. Below it: "Delivery to Texas" and an "Installation Requirements" link.
- **3D tools:** a 3D / Top View switch, and a toolbar to reset, screenshot, rotate left and right, zoom, undo and redo. Also "View in your space" (AR), Share, Saved Designs with a login, a View Details summary, Help, a chat bubble, and scene backgrounds (Backyard, Basic Gray, Farm).

### Options
- **Style:** Standard, Single Slope, Barn, Open Porch. Roof pitch 2/12 to 6/12. Overhangs none, 2 ft or 3 ft. Gutters and downspouts (priced, not drawn). Concrete work yes or no. "Pre-Engineered Upgrade" yes or no. Use: utility, residential or commercial.
- **Sides & Ends:** wall coverage, a center storage room ("Add Storage"), lean-tos on all four sides, and sections you can drag.
- **Size:** width, length and eave height per section.
- **Interior:** frame spacing 20 ft or 25 ft, priced but not drawn.
- **Materials:** PBR siding. Insulation: none, 1 in spray foam R-7, 2 in spray foam R-13, fiberglass R-10.
- **Colors:** roof, trim, wainscot and walls.

### On a phone
- The 3D view sits on top with a gray pull handle under it. Dragging the handle down hides the panel completely, so the building fills the screen with only the handle and the Quote bar left. Dragging up brings the panel back to any height.
- Section tabs scroll sideways and get cut off ("Doors & Win…"), the same problem our step bar has.

### Quote form
- Eight required questions: how you heard about them, concrete, permits, use, site ready, how soon, and the kind of salesperson you prefer. Optional: price range, which salesperson, notes.
- After sending, the customer gets an email with the breakdown but no prices. A priced proposal follows "within 1–2 business days". The form also offers "Print PDF".

## Weaknesses & gaps
- No price at all, against Triple J's "From $X".
- A long, qualifying quote form, against Triple J's name, phone and ZIP.
- Proposal in 1–2 business days, against Triple J's reply within 24 hours.
- A forced location box before anything else, and a login to save designs.

## Ideas for `/build`
1. Let customers open any section; keep "Next" as a guide, not a gate.
2. Highlight the part being edited on the building, and let customers tap a wall to add a door.
3. On phones, a drag handle that resizes or fully hides the panel.
4. Doors: pick the wall first, then add.
5. Keep the quote one tap away without competing with "Next".
6. Triple J builds what their options list (gutters, storage rooms, lean-tos on all four sides, fiberglass insulation): see `Business Profile.md` and `Decisions.md` 2026-10-17.
