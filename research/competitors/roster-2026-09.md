## Method and its limits — read before quoting anything here

| | |
|---|---|
| **Baseline** | `.firecrawl/search-*.json`, captured **2026-04-13** (established from git history, not file mtime) — six city SERPs + 10 scraped competitor sites |

| **Price basis** | Steel + install, **concrete excluded**, matching the sales-pack basis. Competitor figures that bundle concrete are marked `est` |

**What this data cannot tell you, stated plainly:**

- **Google review counts are mostly missing.** Search snippets did not expose them for the local operators.
  Only figures with a source below are real; everything else needs a manual Google Maps pass. This is the
  single biggest hole in the file.
- **The local 3-pack is not represented at all.** No tool available here returns it. Pack occupancy per city
  is unmeasured, and for a local trades business the pack is where the clicks are.
- **April vs September SERPs are not a like-for-like comparison.** April came from Firecrawl's search API,
  September from a different engine with no Temple geolocation. Rank *movement* between the two is therefore
  not a supportable claim, and none is made below. Both are recorded as separate observations.
- **No competitor ad data.** Meta Ad Library and Google Ads Transparency were not successfully queried.
  The military ad audit is incomplete.

## Tier A profiles

### 1. ProStructures — the real Tier 1 threat
- **Model:** manufactures and installs. 16+ years, 12 Texas locations. `prostructures.com`
- **Local presence:** Belton/Temple operation, Joseph Zeluff, 5500 W FM 93 Temple, 254-581-6690 (`FB`, Apr 2026)
- **Reviews:** **194 Google reviews, 4.8★** — *company-wide figure from a search summary; not separately
  verified for the Belton branch.* Even discounted, this is two orders of magnitude above Triple J.
- **Head-to-head (Apr 2026, now stale):** 30×50×14 enclosed garage — building $21,105 + concrete $12,750 = **$33,855**
- **Gaps:** no welded-vs-bolted differentiation, no timeline claim, no Spanish, no military angle
- **Why it matters:** the only competitor that beats Triple J on *every* trust signal — tenure, review volume,
  footprint — while being weaker on every *positioning* axis.

### 2. Central Texas Metal Buildings — owns the category phrase, and welds
- **Owner:** Abel Zubieta · **Founded 1995** · 30+ employees · 506 Cori Dr, Belton TX 76513
- **Reviews:** 19 (Birdeye). Yelp listing updated Aug 2026.
- **Welding:** customer reviews describe "proper welding and bolting of buildings to foundations" — **they weld.**
- **Licence:** BuildZoom shows the GC licence **expired 2021-05-28**, status verified 2026-03-02.
- **Name collision:** distinct from `carportsandmetalbuildingsctx.com`, which trades as **Texas Metal Buildings, LLC**
  (254-613-5024, serves Belton/Waco/Georgetown/Round Rock/Austin, on-site assembly included, no welding claim).
  Two different businesses, near-identical search footprint. See `central-texas-builders.md`.
- **Why it matters:** 30 years, 30+ employees, in Belton, and welds. The nearest thing to a true peer.

| Claim | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Welded or bolted** | ⚠ **WEAKENED** | At least four in-market competitors weld: Central Texas Metal Buildings (Belton, reviews describe welding), Polo's (Waco→Temple, "clean welds"), Laneways (Harker Heights, certified welders), Hill Country Mobile Welding (*"Welded, not bolted, and built by hand"* — covers **Georgetown and Round Rock**, two Triple J location pages). The claim is still true of *national kit dealers*. It is **not** true of the local field. |
| **Same-week install** | ✅ **HOLDS** | Verified competitor lead times: Get Carports **4–8 wks**, Dayton Barns **4–8 wks**, Mayberry **4–6 wks**, Cardinal **2–4 wks**, Lone Star "a couple months". Hill Country claims 2–4 *days* but is out of Triple J's core market. |
| **Zero subcontractors** | ✅ **HOLDS (unverifiable externally)** | No competitor makes a comparable claim; national dealers explicitly route through dealer/installer networks. | **Hablamos Español** | ❌ **FALSE AS PUBLISHED** | Polo's Carports runs a full English/Español site and serves Temple, Belton, and Killeen. The live comparison row marking this Triple J-only is wrong. The April "uncontested" finding rested on a five-listing Marketplace sample. |

**Turnkey** is not re-litigated here. `Locked Decisions.md` settled it 2026-05-02: turnkey is a real offering,
usable in copy whenever no price is attached. Competitive support for it is strong — of every competitor checked,
only L&E Metal also sells concrete, and no national dealer does. **This is the strongest surviving differentiator
and the field evidence backs leaning on it harder, not less.**

## Price positioning — range level

On the steel + install basis, the post-raise sheet sits in the **lower-middle of the local installed market and
above the national kit floor.** Precisely:

- **Below** ProStructures on comparable enclosed structures (the one real April head-to-head had Triple J ~8% under).
- **Above** the national kit "from" prices (Get Carports $1,695 carport / $5,370 garage) — but those are smallest-size
  base units with no concrete, no permit, and a 4–8 week wait, so they are an anchor, not a competitor quote.
- **Not comparable** to Lone Star's published portable-building prices — different product category.

The 13–15% raise did **not** move Triple J out of competitive range. No repricing is indicated by this data.

**Competitor concrete pricing stays internal** (ProStructures $12,750 on a 30×50 pad, Apr 2026) — per the
2026-05-02 lock, never published, quote by call or DM only.

**Financing is the real pricing gap.** Get Carports, The Carport Co., Lone Star, and Leland's all offer
rent-to-own or financing; several advertise $0 down and no credit check. Triple J offers none. For a Fort Cavazos
buyer on BAH timing, monthly payment often beats sticker price.

**Reading:** city-modified organic is owned almost entirely by **national kit dealers running programmatic
location pages at scale**. Triple J's 14 city + 8 county pages are competing directly against that, and losing.
The realistic openings are (a) the local 3-pack, which is unmeasured and which national dealers cannot occupy
without a local address, and (b) intent queries the kit dealers structurally cannot answer — welded, turnkey
with concrete, same-week, bilingual.

Corroborating (a): the one query where Triple J surfaced at all was the *welded + turnkey* long-tail
("welded carports Central Texas installer Temple/Belton"), where `triplejmetaltx.com` returned **second**,
above Cardinal, DexKnows, and the kit dealers.
