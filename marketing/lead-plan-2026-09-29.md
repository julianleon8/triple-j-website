## The answers

**Google or Facebook?** Put the paid budget into **Google Search**. Use **Facebook for free**:
Marketplace, the Page and groups.

- Someone who types "carport builders Temple TX" plans to buy soon. A Facebook ad reaches people who
  weren't looking.
- $500 a month is too little for Meta's delivery algorithm to learn from. It wants about 50 results a
  week. Google Search works at small budgets because it isn't waiting on that learning.
- Google Ads tracking is already installed and verified on the live site. The site has no Meta pixel.
- Facebook's strongest channel for this trade doesn't cost anything. Capital Metal told us Marketplace is
  its highest-return channel (`Decisions.md` 2026-04-21), and listing there is free.

**Connect Facebook Marketplace to the app?** **No, don't build it.** Marketplace sellers post from
personal profiles, and Meta offers no public API that reads those listings or their messages, so there's
nothing to connect to. The app can already sort Marketplace buyers:

- Put a tracked link in every listing: `triplejmetaltx.com/quote?service=carport&src=fbm`. The form fills
  in the building type, collects ZIP and size, and saves `fbm` as the lead's source. If Marketplace
  rejects a listing because of the link, remove the link and ask for the details in messages instead.
- When someone only sends a message, reply with a saved reply that asks for ZIP, size and phone number.
  Then put the phone number into **HQ → Capture**. A phone number alone is enough, and it takes about
  20 seconds.
- Messages to the Triple J **Page** already reach HQ through the webhook (after the duplicate-lead fix
  above).

**Number one priority:** **Get the Google Business Profile live and collect the first 10 real reviews.**
It's free, and it makes everything else work better: ads, Maps, and Facebook buyers who look you up
before they call. Every other channel here partly makes up for not having it. Ads and Marketplace run
alongside it so leads start coming in while that gets fixed.

### Getting the Google Business Profile back

The cause hasn't been diagnosed yet. Start with the reason Google gave in its removal or suspension
email. Common causes:

- **Keywords in the business name.** The name must be exactly **Triple J Metal**, with no "Buildings,"
  "Temple" or "Carports" added.
- **A visible address customers don't visit.** Set it up as a **service-area business** with the address
  hidden and the service cities listed.
- **More than one profile.** Julian and Juan each creating a profile, or a new profile created after
  every removal, can itself trigger suspension. One Google account should own the one profile.
- **Changing the name, address, phone or category while verification is pending.**

For **video verification**, record one continuous clip showing the street, a branded truck or shop sign,
the tools and equipment (welder, skid-steer), and proof you run the business (LLC paperwork, keys). If
the profile is suspended rather than unverified, file Google's reinstatement request with the Certificate
of Formation and a utility bill or business license.

## Paid: $500/month

### Month 1 (October): $400 Google Search + $100 one-time proof

**Google Search campaign, about $13/day**

- Search Network only. **Display Network off.**
- Location: Temple, Belton, Killeen, Harker Heights. Use "Presence: people in or regularly in," not
  "interested in."
- Schedule: only hours when someone will answer the phone.
- Bidding: Maximize clicks with a max CPC cap. Don't switch to Smart Bidding until the account has about
  15 conversions in 30 days.
- Match types: phrase and exact only.

| Ad group | Keyword themes | Final URL |
|---|---|---|
| Carports & RV covers | carport builder/installer + city, metal carport + city, RV cover builder, carports near me | `/services/carports?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=carports` |
| Garages & shops | metal garage builder, metal shop builder, metal building contractor + city | `/services/metal-garages?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=garages` |

**Negative keywords:** kit, kits, diy, how to, plans, used, jobs, hiring, salary, rent, rental, portable,
canopy, tent, pop up, wood, lowes, home depot, harbor freight, costco. From the old campaign's search
terms: prefab, house, homes, cabin, cabins, quonset, shed, sheds, manufacturer, manufacturers, derksen,
vevor, barndominium, barndominiums, barndo, barndos (barndominium builds are paused until 2027, owner
2026-09-29). Check the search-terms report every week and add new negatives.

**Headlines (30 characters max):** Carport Builders in Temple TX · Welded or Bolted Carports · Same-Week
Installs · 20x20 Carports From $3,000 · Temple-Based Metal Crew · Se Habla Español · Military Discount
Available · Free Quotes, Reply in 24 Hrs

**Descriptions (90 characters max):**
- 20x20 carport, steel + install from $3,000. Concrete available, priced separately.
- Welded or bolted. Temple-based crew, zero subcontractors. Se habla español.
- Same-week installs once materials arrive. Free quote, reply within 24 hours.

The garage ad group carries **no price in a headline**. The $5,500 anchor is the steel-and-install shell,
and walls, doors and concrete come on top, so a headline price would mislead.

**Assets:** call asset, sitelinks (Gallery, Carports, Garages, Get a Quote), callouts (Welded or Bolted,
Same-Week Installs, Temple-Based Crew, Se Habla Español, Military Discount).

**What to expect, roughly.** The old campaign (below) averaged $1.69 a click, but its one Temple keyword
cost $5.18, and the new campaign's tighter local targeting will push the average up. Plan on $2–5 per
click: $400 buys about 80–200 clicks. The old campaign turned 1 of 221 clicks into a tracked lead (0.45%).
Calls weren't tracked then, so the true rate may be higher, but don't assume the usual 5–10% until the new
campaign shows it. At 2–5%, that's about 2–10 leads. Judge the campaign on **cost per won job**, not cost
per click. One $3,000+ carport brings in more than 7× the month's ad spend.

**The old campaign, 2026-04-22 to 05-18** ("Campaign #1", from the owner's all-time Google Ads export,
2026-09-29). It's **Paused** (status reason "campaign paused"), so fixing billing won't restart it.

- Search, $33/day budget. 221 clicks, 2,858 impressions (7.73% CTR), $372.87 spent, $1.69 average per

- **1 conversion, so $372.87 per lead.** Phone calls weren't counted, and form-conversion tracking wasn't
  confirmed live until 2026-09-29.
- The owner reported a peak on April 27 of 166 clicks and 2,052 impressions, most of the run. It's
  probably a weekly figure (April 27 was a Monday). Either way, most of the budget went in about a week.

These five drew 74% of the clicks and 69% of the spend. "Metal Buildings Temple Texas" had the best CTR
(13.64%) and is the only one naming a city. The cheap statewide terms may have drawn clicks from outside the
service area; check the old campaign's locations report. "Cocheras de Metal" shows real Spanish-language
demand, and the site has no Spanish page yet.

**What people searched** (Search terms insight export, same run). Google hid the terms behind 142 of the
221 clicks (64%), including every search under "Cocheras de Metal." Of the 62 clicks with a visible term:

- **About 8 were the right customer:** someone nearby wanting a carport installed, such as "metal carports
  near me installed prices" (4 clicks out of 5 impressions), "carports near me" and "carport builders near
  me."
- **22 wanted a house, barndominium or cabin**, such as "turnkey barndominium builders in texas" (6 clicks).
- **16 wanted a kit, prefab or DIY building.** The campaign's only conversion came from one of these:
  "prefab metal building kits prices."
- **Many were statewide shopping.** A few named Houston, Austin, or East, West or North Texas.

Most of the old campaign's clicks came from people who weren't buying what Triple J sells, where Triple J
works. The four-city presence targeting, phrase and exact match, and the negatives above are the fix.
