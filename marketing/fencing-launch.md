# Triple J Metal — fencing launch

Prepared September 26, 2026. Owner confirmed launch scope: metal privacy fencing, pipe/ranch fencing, ornamental metal fencing, and gates.

## What changes now

Keep the existing brand and building services. Add a dedicated fencing page, navigation and footer links, homepage fencing feature, and a fencing choice in every quote form. The fencing page opens with fencing selected. The short quote link also accepts `?service=fencing`.

Landing page: https://www.triplejmetaltx.com/services/metal-fencing
Direct quote: https://www.triplejmetaltx.com/quote?service=fencing

The form asks for style, approximate linear feet, height, gates, removal, ZIP, contact information, preferred timing and budget. Fencing details are preserved in lead notes under the existing Other category; fencing does not yet have its own dashboard reporting category. No fence pricing is inferred from building pricing. Installation dates are confirmed per project.

Do not wait for a full redesign. Do not spread a small launch budget across every city and ad channel. Begin with the nearby service corridor, then expand based on profitable jobs.

## Local positioning

Proposed positioning: **Metal fencing and gates built around your property by a Temple-based metal crew.**

Keep all three styles visible. Give each style its own search-ad group, matched to the relevant section of the fencing page. A separate gate group can follow if demand warrants it. Start with exact and phrase match for local service searches, then review actual search terms. Example themes:

- metal fence installation Temple TX / metal privacy fence Belton
- pipe fence contractor Temple / ranch fencing Bell County
- ornamental metal fencing Killeen / ornamental fence Harker Heights

Exclude searches clearly about employment, free materials, DIY instructions, or suppliers when they do not fit the service. Target people in the service area; use call assets during hours the team can answer. Track submitted inquiries as leads and phone clicks separately from qualified calls. Use consistent campaign parameters, for example `utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=fencing_launch`.

No paid campaign has been created or funded by this work. The owner still needs to choose a spending cap and confirm available crew capacity.

## Proof to collect from the first jobs

For each style: a finished wide view, entrance/gate view, close-ups of posts and finish, and a short project description with city, approximate footage and scope. Ask for permission before showing identifiable private property. Request an honest review after completion; do not script a positive review or claim a ranking award.

## What “top locally” means

Measure visibility and conversion separately: Search Console fencing impressions and clicks, Business Profile calls/site visits, qualified fencing leads, estimate rate, jobs won, and acquisition cost. Establish the baseline at launch. Rankings vary by searcher location; a page cannot guarantee a top position.
