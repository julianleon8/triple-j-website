# Reference sites (2026-10-01)

The owner asked Claude to propose sites, then say what they like. Screenshots of each homepage:
`reference-sites.jpg` (Kevin Travis's hero video and Lucchese's video did not play in the headless
browser; Rigby and Morton show their popups). These are references for layout and type only: almost
all of them use a red, gold or bronze accent, and ours stays navy, slate and silver.

| # | Site | Look at | Don't copy |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [B&D Builders](https://www.banddbuilders.com/) | Project cards titled "Project, Town, State", the Builds pattern. Serif display over a clean sans on near-black and slate. | The "Download brochure" hero button; the luxury tone. |
| 2 | [Morton Buildings, projects](https://mortonbuildings.com/projects) | Gallery filtered by building use; a model for tagging builds by type. | Red accent, condensed caps, the heavy filter panel. |
| 3 | [Walters Buildings](https://waltersbuildings.com/) | Two-button photo hero; a specs block that builds trust without reviews. | Red accent, the 3D-designer push, their spec numbers. |
| 4 | [Kevin Travis Homes](https://www.kevintravishomes.com/) | The closest match: Texas, Next.js, serif + Inter, centred hero, numbered project slider with "Type · Town" tags, three-card services. | Gold accent; the "few homes a year" boutique pitch. |
| 5 | [Blackstone Builders](https://blackstonebigsky.com/) | Big serif headline over dark photo, primary + secondary buttons, portfolio cards labelled type/location/name, FAQ as trust. | The long keyword headline. |
| 6 | [Hobbs, Inc.](https://hobbsinc.com/) | Charcoal + slate palette with a serif and Inter; a short inquiry form (name, email, location, details). | Popup form, bronze accent, a hero with no button. |
| 7 | [Blue Ribbon Builders](https://blueribbonbuilders.com/) | "Family-owned and operated since 1975" line under the headline: a model for the family story. | Project tiles with no captions. |
| 8 | [John Rigby & Co.](https://www.johnrigbyandco.com/) | Deep navy with classical serifs and steel/engraving photography, the nearest feel to a silver lion on navy. | Page-builder template parts, the newsletter popup. |
| 9 | [Lucchese](https://www.lucchese.com/) | A Texas heritage brand in navy with a Roman display face that looks premium without going "western". | Shop clutter, sign-up popups. |

Research cost: one subagent, about 139k tokens (11 searches, 15 page fetches, about 55 page and
stylesheet downloads); the screenshots were taken separately.
